Most integration tools hide the work behind a canvas. That is fine until a rule is awkward, a source lies, or someone has to change the job six months later. We bring [ETLBox](https://www.etlbox.net) so week one is your sources and your rules — not building a toolkit from scratch. The library has **AI support**. Because the work is code, AI can help us write it, and we can put AI into the flow where it actually pays off.
