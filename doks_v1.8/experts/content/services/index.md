We do not sell a platform you have to learn. We write **code** — usually C# on ETLBox — that fits your sources, your rules, and the way your team already ships software.

## Custom ETL in C#

Bespoke flows when the mapping is ugly, the volume is high, or the source will not sit still. Incremental loads, merges, slowly changing dimensions, late-arriving facts — the work that looks simple on a whiteboard and is not.

You get a repository you can build, test, and change. We stay for the next slice, or we hand it over.

## Training and enablement

Your developers should not depend on us for every new connector. We run focused training on ETLBox and on how we structure testable pipelines: sources, transforms, destinations, and the tests that keep them honest.

Useful when a team is adopting the library, inheriting a flow, or wants a review before the next release.

## Surfaces around the flow

The pipeline is rarely the whole product. Teams ask us for ops screens, sync tools, and small apps that sit on top of the same C# — the kind of surface we ship as [DirectSync](https://www.directsync.net) for our own product line.

Dashboards, retry buttons, mapping UIs, admin APIs. Same codebase, same tests.

## APIs and system integration

REST, SOAP, GraphQL, files, queues, and the databases behind them. Extract from one system, shape the payload, load into another. We treat an API as a first-class source or destination — not an afterthought bolted onto a warehouse job.

## Warehouse, lake, or vault

When the project needs a home for the data, we build that too: a classic warehouse, a lake for raw history, or a vault-style model when the sources keep changing. We pick the shape that matches how you query — we do not start from a methodology slide.

## How we deliver

- **Proof of concept at no cost** — a real slice of your integration, not a demo dataset.
- **Fixed price** for the full delivery once the scope is clear.
- **Your environment or ours** — on-prem, Kubernetes, Azure Functions, App Service, or anywhere .NET runs.
- **Self-managed or hosted** — you run the jobs, or we keep them running.
